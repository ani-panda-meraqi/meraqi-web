What the matrix shows

  • Meraqi is the only vendor in this set that prices in outcomes. Every competitor sells an abstract pool (credits or tokens) whose real-world yield depends on which model you pick; the buyer does the arithmetic. Meraqi states deliverables per month, minutes of finished video and images, counted from each user's own billing date with no daily limits, so within the month nothing is use-it-or-lose-it.
  • Units are not comparable across vendors. Higgsfield, Artlist, OpenArt and InVideo meter credits per month, each with different model costs per credit; Magnific meters credits per year; Leonardo meters tokens with a rollover bank. A "credits" column that looks bigger is not necessarily more output.
  • Video-first pricing has reset. InVideo replaced its $17 to $900 credit plans with per-seat plans at $20, $50 and $101 a month billed annually ($20, $60 and $150 monthly). By its own count the $20 Starter seat covers about 15 short Seedance 2.0 Fast clips a month; Meraqi Starter, at $12, includes 60 minutes of video creation.
  • Annual-vs-monthly billing splits obscure headline prices. Magnific Pro is $210 or $280, Artlist AI Creator is $41.67 or $69.99, and InVideo Max is $101 or $150 depending on the toggle. Meraqi leads with the monthly price everywhere and states annual billing plainly as two months free.
  • Compliance is gated up-tier everywhere. SSO, indemnification and SOC 2 live in team or contact-sales tiers across the matrix; the cheapest SSO in the set is Magnific's $55/seat Business (minimum 2 seats), and InVideo's starts with its $79/seat Team plan (minimum 5 seats). A published Self-Hosted tier (on-prem, gov cloud or air-gapped, built for regulated industries) has no counterpart anywhere in this set, and every Meraqi plan includes passwordless sign-in with inherited two-step verification, including single-seat Starter (no passwords stored; Google and Microsoft today, with company logins through the Meraqi Identity Broker on the way), while competitors sell identity features as upsells.
  • Seat prices sit mid-market. Business at $50/seat undercuts Magnific Business ($55-69/seat) and InVideo Team ($79/seat, minimum 5 seats) and sits just above Leonardo's $48 Growth seat. What a seat buys differs, which is what the calculator below is for.
  • "Unlimited" claims carry fine print. Higgsfield sells 7-day unlimited windows per model; Leonardo's unlimited is relaxed-queue on selected models; Magnific and Artlist make selected models unlimited, and Artlist reserves all models for Custom Business. Meraqi's free month avoids the word entirely; it is a plainly stated pool, 10 minutes of video creation and 300 images, with a watermark and a relaxed queue, and it needs no card.
