Meraqiμεράκι · meraki

noun · Greek origin

To do something with soul, creativity, and love. To leave a piece of yourself in the work.

We named the platform after the feeling we wanted your work to keep, even when AI handles the heavy lifting. Speed should not cost you your passion and creativity.

Why I built Meraqi

It started with a message to my daughter.

One morning I got the news that my daughter had done really well at school. I wanted to send her something that matched the moment. A personal, uplifting note, designed in a style I knew she would love.

I went looking for a tool that could do it simply and quickly. I couldn't find one. So I sent a plain text message instead, and it never quite sat right with me.

I'm an engineer by trade, but I've always loved art and design. That morning I thought, I can build this. Not just for me, but for everyone who has something to say and wants it to look and feel like they mean it.

That is Meraqi.

- Ani Panda, Founder

How it works

Four steps from idea to finish.

The scale and scope of creativity is practically boundless. A personalized greeting card. A book cover. An ad campaign, or a clip for social media, built and ready to post. Type a prompt to create an image, then add a QR code that links anywhere. A wedding invitation with directions to the venue, the map link turned into a scannable code right on the card. Share a link to your product or marketing page and get a video for the big announcement. Load your meeting notes, a voice recording and your financial statements, and get back a presentation ready for new clients (coming soon). And the list goes on and on!

Ask for an image, a video, or 3D graphics (coming soon). Meraqi plans it and makes it, you refine it, and every version is kept.

01

Describe it

Type it, say it, or start from a reference image or a reference video (coming soon). For a video, you can also paste a web page link. Meraqi rewrites your prompt for better results, and you can edit the rewrite first.

02

Meraqi makes it

For an image, Gemma 4 lays out canvas, hierarchy, palette, and type as a DesignSpec, and you see the plan before anything renders. A video comes back as continuous footage, with speech.

03

Refine it

Pick the best of four images, then change the canvas color, edit the text, remove the background, regenerate, apply the Noir Effect, or add an accent, until it's perfect. Advanced editing adds masks and compositing on any layer. Refine a video by re-creating it with a better prompt or reference.

04

Resize for every channel

Magic Resize reflows your image or video to 13+ formats, ready to post. You can also set the size before generating. Publish directly to each channel (coming soon).

Video

The same soul, in motion.

Meraqi keeps a moving scene consistent from frame to frame, so your spot plays like one continuous shot, not a slideshow.

01

Full scenes, with speech

People, products, camera moves, lighting, and a voice track, in up to 90 seconds of continuous 1080p video.

02

A link becomes an ad

Paste your product or marketing page. Meraqi reads it once, plans the spot, and renders it. Your page content is used for that one job, then discarded, never trained on.

03

Consistency is the hard part

Long videos are usually built from segments joined together. Meraqi's engine holds the segments together, keeping the same people, style, and light from one second to the next.

What makes Meraqi different

AI speed, with every layer editable and every file yours.

True multi-layer compositions

Every element is a real layer you can select, edit, and reorder. No regenerating the whole image to move one thing.

Your data stays yours

Meraqi-hosted open-weight models, no third-party API. Your prompts, brand kits, and designs never train any model, ours or anyone else's. And deletion is real. Anything you delete is permanently removed after a 30-day recovery window.
