§ Generation time, what it means: the minutes are how long our GPUs work on your video, not the length of the finished clip. What a clip costs to generate depends on what you are making, the quality you ask for, how long it runs and which model does the work, so the two are not a fixed ratio. Because the models run on our own hardware rather than through a third-party API billed by the output second, the cost is our own measured compute, so we price it from what we actually observe instead of passing through a rate someone else sets and can change. As a guide, a 15 second clip at 720p currently takes 60 to 90 seconds of generation time, so a Starter month is around 40 to 60 of them.

† Self-Hosted, what it means: the platform is deployed inside the customer's own network rather than as shared SaaS, either on the customer's own hardware (on-prem) or in their private or government cloud tenancy, reachable from the intranet only, up to fully air-gapped installations with no internet connectivity. All models ship with the deployment and run locally; no third-party APIs are called, and no prompts, content, or telemetry ever leave the customer's environment. Updates arrive as offline packages, on your schedule. Signed packages are planned. Quotas are unlimited because generation runs on customer-owned GPUs, so capacity is the deployment's hardware rather than shared infrastructure. Storage is sized to your hardware, up to 1 PB per deployment; larger by arrangement.

Processing tiers: your place in the queue, not render speed. To keep delivery fast for everyone, each account runs a limited number of generations at the same time, so a very large batch queues behind itself and takes longer to finish rather than jumping ahead of other customers. Turnaround estimates describe individual jobs as they start, not an entire batch.

Monthly pools, on your own clock: every allowance is a monthly pool that resets on your personal billing date, the day you signed up or upgraded, not the calendar month. There are no daily limits; skip a quiet week, or spend a month's minutes in one afternoon. When a trial month ends, your work stays viewable and downloadable; pick a plan to keep creating. An account with no paid plan closes after 12 months with no sign-in and its content is erased, and we email you before that happens.

What a minute costs: per minute of finished 1080p video that is 20 cents on Starter, 10 cents on Pro and 8 cents on Business. Generation runs on our own GPUs with open models and no per-call API fees, which is why the numbers are a fraction of credit-based tools; the full comparison is below.

Annual billing, all paid tiers: "Sign up for the whole year, get two months free." Starter $120, Pro $250, Business $500 per seat per year; exactly two months free on every tier. Monthly price is always the headline price.

Private beta: the beta program is first come, first served, closing at 1000 user sign-ups, with a public waitlist beyond that. During the beta you get privileged access in exchange for your patronage and feedback, and the option to sign up for Pro for life at $125/year instead of $250, for as long as the subscription stays active. Cancel anytime; access runs through the end of the paid year.
