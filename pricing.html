Processing tiers set queue position, not render speed: video generation is compute-bound at roughly 3-4 minutes per 45 seconds of finished footage on production hardware. "Seconds" delivery applies to images only.

Monthly pools, on your own clock. Every allowance is a monthly pool that resets on your personal billing date, the day you signed up or upgraded, not the calendar month. There are no daily limits; skip a quiet week, or spend a month's minutes in one afternoon. When a trial month ends, your work stays viewable and downloadable; pick a plan to keep creating. ‡

What a minute costs. Per minute of finished 1080p video that is 20 cents on Starter, 10 cents on Pro and 8 cents on Business. Generation runs on our own GPUs with open models and no per-call API fees, which is why the numbers are a fraction of credit-based tools; the full comparison is below.

Annual billing, all paid tiers: "Sign up for the whole year, get two months free." Starter $120, Pro $250, Business $500 per seat per year; exactly two months free on every tier. Monthly price is always the headline price.

Private beta, closes 12/31/2026. The first 500 guests get in, first come, first served, with a public waitlist beyond that; a second wave opens once production capacity is live. During the beta you get privileged access in exchange for your patronage and feedback. When the beta ends, guests have the option to sign up for Pro for life at $150/year instead of $250, for as long as the subscription stays active. Cancel anytime; access runs through the end of the paid year.

† Self-Hosted, what it means: the platform is deployed inside the customer's own network rather than as shared SaaS: on the customer's own hardware (on-prem) or in their private or government cloud tenancy, reachable from the intranet only, up to fully air-gapped installations with no internet connectivity. All models ship with the deployment and run locally; no third-party APIs are called, and no prompts, content, or telemetry ever leave the customer's environment. Updates arrive as signed offline packages. Quotas are unlimited because generation runs on customer-owned GPUs: capacity is the deployment's hardware, not shared infrastructure. Storage is sized to your hardware, up to 1 PB per deployment; larger by arrangement. Priced per deployment.

‡ Pacing and fair use: your monthly pool is yours to spend on any schedule, including all at once. To keep delivery fast for everyone, each account runs a limited number of generations at the same time; a very large batch simply queues behind itself and finishes over hours rather than jumping ahead of other customers. Turnaround estimates describe individual jobs as they start, not an entire batch.
