The problem

Frozen pixels for users. Broken margins for the builders.

For the user

Un-editable and soulless

Type a prompt, get a flat image baked into pixels. Change one word or move one thing? Regenerate and pray. It never feels like yours.

For the builder · the API tax

Every click costs them money

99% of consumer AI apps are thin wrappers on OpenAI, Anthropic, or Midjourney, paying a per-token fee for every generation. Go viral and heavy users detonate the margins.

The death trap

Built on someone else's meter

One price change, model update, or banned key from a frontier provider, and the app dies overnight. No control, no moat, no business.

Consumers get frozen pixels; the startups serving them are built on someone else's meter.

The infrastructure moat · the tech math

We own the stack. That's the whole business.

Decoupled costs

Flat capacity, not per-token

We pay predictable monthly GPU capacity we control, not a variable fee for every image or token. No frontier-API tax on every click.

Margins that hold

80-85% gross, at scale

Infra is our main cost, and it is capacity-based and predictable. Heavy users who generate thousands of assets don't drain the company.

The LTV / CAC engine

Outspend the wrappers

A $25/mo subscription stays lucrative even for power users, so we have the financial leverage to outspend API-taxed competitors on acquisition.

When a wrapper goes viral, it bleeds. When we go viral, we compound.
