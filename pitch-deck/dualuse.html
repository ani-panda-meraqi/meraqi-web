The technology moat · NSF + DoD SBIR tracks

Two inventions. One moat.

A consistency framework makes independently-trained open models behave as one coherent engine; a deterministic execution protocol makes that engine impossible to crash from load. Both live outside the models, with no retraining.

Compositional consistency

Cross-modal latent anchoring plus long-horizon streaming: an identity and spatial prior corrects drift at every model hand-off, and the anchored latent tail re-injects across windows so long video holds together.

Deterministic admission · ADR-0021

Every request reserves a pre-measured memory envelope against a per-device ledger before dispatch. Out-of-memory becomes queue, degrade, or reject, never a crash; formally specified, with a provable containment property.

Self-supervising closed loop

Observed memory peaks feed back: drift detection re-calibrates and demotes profiles automatically, and a calibrated fallback ladder degrades at admission, never mid-deliverable. No manual tuning in the field.

The before and after

The identical 45-second workload that died out-of-memory at six seconds before the streaming redesign now completes, picture and synchronized voice, on one 24 GB consumer GPU.

Model-agnostic by design: model families are calibration data, never code. Every better open model composes in; we turn commodity open models into a compliant production system.
