About Meraqi
The unmet need

Creative and media directors in regulated industries (banks, insurers, healthcare, defense, government) are locked out of the GenAI creative wave; every frontier model is a third-party cloud API, and their briefs, brand assets, customer data, and unreleased campaigns are not allowed to leave their environment; the teams under the most pressure to produce more content faster are precisely the ones who cannot touch the tools everyone else uses.

What Meraqi is

A GenAI design platform, named for the Greek meráki (“to create with soul”), built to give those teams the full creative stack; plain language becomes finished marketing content, images, multi-shot video ads with speech and sound, and soon decks and 3D, created and edited in any browser.

Sovereign by design

The entire platform ships self-hosted, up to fully air-gapped, inside the customer’s own walls; local open-weight models only, zero telemetry, nothing ever leaves the environment, signed offline updates; a creative department gets modern generative tooling without asking compliance to bend a single rule.

The technology that makes it possible

A compositional consistency engine keeps people, products, and scenes coherent across a full spot (45 seconds of continuous video+audio proven, roughly 6 minutes wall-clock per ad), and deterministic GPU scheduling keeps long generation runs reliable on modest customer-owned hardware; no frontier-API dependence, no per-call tax.

Business and stage

The only vendor in its field with a published Regulated Industries tier, an on-prem license metered by VRAM (~$75k/yr baseline, unlimited seats), alongside outcome-priced SaaS at $12/$25/$50 per seat/month; solo-founder pre-seed raising $750K, with a signed pilot LOI and NSF SBIR and DoD/AFWERX tracks in flight.

Elegant, Simple, Sovereign and Secure
